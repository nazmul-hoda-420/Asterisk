Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

1. Who we are

This is a call-center and customer-communication platform. This policy explains what information we collect when handling calls, SMS, and WhatsApp messages on behalf of the business operating this platform, and how that information is used.

2. Information we collect

  • Phone numbers and names of callers and message senders
  • Call metadata (time, duration, outcome) and, where enabled, call recordings
  • WhatsApp and SMS message content exchanged with our agents
  • Basic account information for staff who use this platform (name, extension, login activity)

3. How we use this information

Information is used solely to provide customer support and respond to inquiries: routing calls and messages to the right agent or team, keeping a record of past conversations so agents have context, and reviewing call/chat quality for training purposes. We do not sell this information to third parties.

4. WhatsApp messaging

When you message our business number on WhatsApp, your messages are received through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and stored on our systems so an agent can reply to you, including outside of Meta's own retention window. Meta's own privacy policy also applies to how WhatsApp itself handles your data in transit.

5. Data retention

We retain call records, recordings, and message history for as long as reasonably needed for support, quality, and record-keeping purposes, and delete or anonymize it thereafter.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to stop contacting you, or to delete information we hold about you, by contacting the business through the same phone number or WhatsApp number you used to reach us.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the business operating this platform through their published support number.
